# LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF DANIEL SMITH

I, Daniel Smith, a resident of the State of Maryland, being of sound mind and disposing memory, hereby declare this document to be my Last Will and Testament.

## ARTICLE I – IDENTIFICATION

I am married to my wife, Sarah Smith, and have two children, Michael Smith and Emily Smith.

## ARTICLE II – REVOCATION OF PRIOR WILLS

I hereby revoke all prior wills and codicils previously executed by me.

## ARTICLE III – APPOINTMENT OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I appoint my wife, Sarah Smith, to serve as Personal Representative of my estate. If she is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint my brother, Brian Smith, as successor Personal Representative.

## ARTICLE IV – DISTRIBUTION OF PROPERTY

I direct that my personal property, financial accounts, and remaining estate be distributed equally between my two children, Michael Smith and Emily Smith.

I specifically leave my residence to my wife, Sarah Smith, provided she survives me.

## ARTICLE V – GUARDIANSHIP

If both my wife and I are deceased while either of our children remains a minor, I nominate my sister, Rebecca Smith, to serve as guardian.

## ARTICLE VI – RESIDUARY ESTATE

I give all remaining property of my estate to my wife, Sarah Smith, if she survives me. Otherwise, the residuary estate shall pass equally to my children.

## ARTICLE VII – SIGNATURE

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have signed this Last Will and Testament on November 21, 2026.

____________________________  
Daniel Smith, Testator
