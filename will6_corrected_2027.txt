# LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT
**Date: January 1, 2027**

I, **Katherine Marie Reynolds**, residing in the Commonwealth of Virginia, being of sound mind and disposing memory, do hereby declare this document to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils made by me.

## ARTICLE I – DECLARATION

I declare that I am at least eighteen years of age and that I am making this Will voluntarily, without undue influence, duress, or coercion.

## ARTICLE II – FAMILY INFORMATION

I am married to **Phillip Andrew Richardson**. I have two children:

1. **Emilly Grace Richardson**
2. **Britteny Nicole Richardson**

All references to my children shall include the individuals named above and any children subsequently born to or legally adopted by me.

## ARTICLE III – APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTOR

I hereby nominate and appoint my husband, **Phillip Andrew Richardson**, as Executor of this Last Will and Testament.

If he is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate **Stephen William Richardson** as alternate Executor.

My Executor shall have all powers permitted under applicable law to administer my estate, pay outstanding obligations, distribute assets, and carry out the provisions of this Will.

## ARTICLE IV – PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Executor to pay my legally enforceable debts, funeral expenses, expenses of administering my estate, and any applicable taxes from the assets of my estate as soon as reasonably practicable following my death.

## ARTICLE V – DISTRIBUTION OF PERSONAL PROPERTY

I give all of my tangible personal property, including jewelry, furniture, clothing, automobiles, household furnishings, and personal belongings, to my husband, **Phillip Andrew Richardson**, if he survives me by thirty days.

If my husband does not survive me by thirty days, such property shall be divided equally between my children, **Emilly Grace Richardson** and **Britteny Nicole Richardson**.

## ARTICLE VI – DISTRIBUTION OF RESIDUARY ESTATE

I give, devise, and bequeath the remainder of my estate, including all real property, bank accounts, investments, and other assets not otherwise specifically distributed under this Will, as follows:

- Fifty percent (50%) to my husband, **Phillip Andrew Richardson**, provided he survives me by thirty days.
- Twenty-five percent (25%) to my daughter, **Emilly Grace Richardson**.
- Twenty-five percent (25%) to my daughter, **Britteny Nicole Richardson**.

If my husband fails to survive me by thirty days, his share shall be divided equally between my surviving children.

If either child predeceases me, that child's share shall pass to that child's surviving descendants, per stirpes. If that child leaves no surviving descendants, the share shall pass to my other surviving child or her descendants, per stirpes.

## ARTICLE VII – GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR CHILDREN

If, at the time of my death, any of my children are minors and their other parent is unable or unwilling to serve as guardian, I nominate **Megan Elizabeth Saunders** as guardian of the person of my minor children.

If Megan Elizabeth Saunders is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate **Jessica Anne Williams** as alternate guardian.

## ARTICLE VIII – EXECUTOR'S POWERS

My Executor shall have the authority to:

1. Collect and manage all estate assets.
2. Sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of estate property when reasonably necessary.
3. Pay debts, taxes, and expenses of administration.
4. Distribute property in cash or in kind.
5. Employ attorneys, accountants, and other professionals as reasonably necessary.
6. Take all other lawful actions reasonably necessary to administer my estate.

## ARTICLE IX – NO CONTEST PROVISION

To the extent permitted by applicable law, any beneficiary who directly or indirectly contests this Will without probable cause shall forfeit any interest in my estate that would otherwise pass to that beneficiary.

## ARTICLE X – GOVERNING LAW

This Will shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

## ARTICLE XI – SIGNATURE

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I, **Katherine Marie Reynolds**, hereby sign this Last Will and Testament on the 1st day of January, 2027, declaring it to be my Will and intending that it be given full legal effect.
