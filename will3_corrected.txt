# LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT
## OF DANIEL ROBERT WITAKER

**DATED JULY 3, 2055**

I, **Daniel Robert Witaker**, a resident of the District of Columbia, being of sound mind and disposing memory, hereby make, publish, and declare this instrument to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils made by me.

## ARTICLE I
### DECLARATION AND FAMILY INFORMATION

**1.1 Declaration.** I declare that I am executing this Last Will and Testament voluntarily on July 3, 2055.

**1.2 Family Information.** I am married to Margaret Anne Whitaker. I have two children:

- Michael Daniel Whitaker.
- Chalotte Elizabeth Whitaker.

Any reference in this Will to my children shall include the individuals identified above and any additional children subsequently born to or legally adopted by me.

## ARTICLE II
### PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Personal Representative to pay my legally enforceable debts, funeral expenses, expenses of administering my estate, and any taxes properly payable from my estate as soon as reasonably practicable following my death.

## ARTICLE III
### DISPOSITION OF TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY

I give all of my tangible personal property, including household furnishings, jewelry, vehicles, artwork, and personal effects, to my surviving spouse, Margaret Anne Whitaker.

If my spouse does not survive me, such property shall be divided equally among my surviving children.

## ARTICLE IV
### DISTRIBUTION OF RESIDUARY ESTATE

**4.1 Spousal Distribution.** If my spouse survives me by thirty (30) days, I give fifty percent (50%) of my residuary estate to Margaret Anne Whitaker, outright and free of trust.

**4.2 Children's Distribution.** I give the remaining fifty percent (50%) of my residuary estate in equal shares to my children who survive me.

**4.3 Distribution by Representation.** If either of my children predeceases me but leaves surviving descendants, that child's share shall pass to such descendants by right of representation.

**4.4 No Surviving Spouse.** If my spouse does not survive me by thirty (30) days, my entire residuary estate shall be distributed equally among my surviving children, by right of representation.

## ARTICLE V
### TESTAMENTARY TRUST FOR MINOR BENEFICIARIES

**5.1 Establishment.** Any share passing to a beneficiary who has not attained the age of thirty (30) years shall be held in a separate trust administered by my Personal Representative or a designated Trustee.

**5.2 Distributions.** The Trustee may distribute income and principal for the beneficiary's health, education, maintenance, and support.

**5.3 Final Distribution.** Upon attaining thirty (30) years of age, the beneficiary shall receive the remaining principal and undistributed income.

## ARTICLE VI
### APPOINTMENT OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

**6.1 Initial Appointment.** I nominate my spouse, Margaret Anne Whitaker, to serve as Personal Representative of my estate.

**6.2 Successor Appointment.** If Margaret Anne Whitaker is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate my son, Michael Daniel Whitaker, as successor Personal Representative.

**6.3 Powers.** My Personal Representative shall have all powers permitted under applicable law to administer my estate, including the authority to collect assets, pay expenses, sell property, settle claims, and distribute estate assets.

**6.4 Bond.** I request that no bond be required of any Personal Representative serving under this Will, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## ARTICLE VII
### GENERAL PROVISIONS

**7.1 Survivorship.** Any beneficiary who fails to survive me by thirty (30) days shall be deemed to have predeceased me for purposes of this Will.

**7.2 Governing Law.** This Will shall be governed by the laws of the District of Columbia.

**7.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Will is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**7.4 No Contest.** To the extent permitted by applicable law, any beneficiary who directly contests the validity of this Will shall forfeit any benefit otherwise provided to that beneficiary.

## ARTICLE VIII
### EXECUTION

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I, Daniel Robert Witaker, have signed this Last Will and Testament on July 4, 2055, declaring it to be my Last Will and Testament in the presence of the undersigned witnesses.
