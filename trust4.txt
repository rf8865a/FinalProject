# IRREVOCABLE TRUST AGREEMENT

**THE BENNETT FAMILY IRREVOCABLE TRUST**

**Dated: August 16, 2096**

This Irrevocable Trust Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on August 16, 2096, by and between Steven Bennett, of Arlington, Virginia ("Grantor"), and Michele Reynolds, of Alexandria, Virginia ("Trustee").

## ARTICLE I
### ESTABLISHMENT OF TRUST

1.1 Creation. The Grantor hereby establishes an irrevocable trust to be known as the Bennett Family Irrevocable Trust ("Trust").

1.2 Transfer of Property. The Grantor transfers and delivers the property identified in Schedule A to the Trustee, to be held and administered according to this Agreement.

1.3 Irrevocability. This Trust shall be irrevocable upon execution. The Grantor shall have no unilateral authority to revoke or amend this Agreement, except as permitted by applicable law.

## ARTICLE II
### BENEFICIARIES

2.1 Primary Beneficiaries.The beneficiaries of this Trust shall be:

- Stephen Bennett, son of the Grantor.
- Sally Bennett, daughter of the Grantor.
- Sara Reynolds, granddaughter of the Grantor.
- Philip Bennett, nephew of the Grantor.

2.2 Beneficial Interests. Each beneficiary shall have an interest in the Trust subject to the distribution provisions and discretionary powers established herein.

## ARTICLE III
### TRUSTEE POWERS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Administration. The Trustee, Michele Reynolds, shall administer the Trust in accordance with its terms and applicable fiduciary standards.

3.2 Investment Authority. The Trustee may invest Trust assets in stocks, bonds, mutual funds, real estate, and other investments deemed appropriate.

3.3 Discretionary Distributions. The Trustee may distribute income or principal for the health, education, maintenance, and support of Stephen Bennett, Michelle Bennett, Sara Reynolds, and Philip Bennett.

3.4 Successor Trustee. If Michele Reynolds resigns, becomes incapacitated, or otherwise ceases to serve, **Dennis MacDonald** shall serve as successor Trustee.

## ARTICLE IV
### DISTRIBUTION OF TRUST PROPERTY

4.1 During Grantor's Lifetime. The Trustee may make discretionary distributions to the beneficiaries in accordance with Article III.

4.2 Upon Grantor's Death. Upon the death of Steven Bennett, the Trustee shall continue administering the Trust for the benefit of the beneficiaries.

4.3 Final Distribution. When the youngest living primary beneficiary reaches thirty (30) years of age, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining Trust principal and accumulated income in equal shares among the then-living primary beneficiaries.

## ARTICLE V
### SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

5.1 No beneficiary may voluntarily assign, pledge, transfer, or encumber any interest in the Trust before actual distribution.

5.2 To the extent permitted by applicable law, no undistributed Trust interest shall be subject to attachment, execution, or garnishment by a beneficiary's creditors.

## ARTICLE VI
### TRUSTEE COMPENSATION AND ACCOUNTING

6.1 **Compensation.** The Trustee shall receive reasonable compensation for services performed in administering the Trust.

6.2 **Records.** The Trustee shall maintain accurate records of all receipts, disbursements, investments, and distributions.

6.3 **Accountings.** The Trustee shall provide accountings to qualified beneficiaries as required by applicable law.

## ARTICLE VII
### GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

7.2 The courts of the Commonwealth of Virginia shall have jurisdiction over matters concerning the interpretation and administration of this Trust, subject to applicable law.

## ARTICLE VIII
### MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

8.1 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

8.2 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement concerning the establishment and administration of this Trust.

8.3 **Effective Date.** This Trust shall become effective upon execution and transfer of the initial Trust property.
