# REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST AGREEMENT

**THE WHITCOMBE FAMILY REVOCABLE TRUST**

**Dated: June 29, 2023**

This Revocable Living Trust Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into this 29th day of June, 2023, by **Geoffrey Whitcomb**, residing in Bethesda, Maryland (the "Grantor").

## ARTICLE I  
### ESTABLISHMENT OF TRUST

The Grantor hereby establishes this Revocable Living Trust, to be known as the **Whitcombe Family Revocable Trust**, and transfers to the Trustee the property described in Schedule A attached hereto.

The Grantor intends that this Trust provide for the management, preservation, and distribution of his assets during his lifetime and following his death.

## ARTICLE II  
### TRUSTEE

The Grantor shall initially serve as the Trustee of this Trust. In the event that the Grantor becomes incapacitated, resigns, or otherwise ceases to serve, **Katherine Mclaughlin** shall serve as Successor Trustee.

If Katherine Mclaughlin is unable or unwilling to serve, **Stephen Pritchard** shall serve as alternate Successor Trustee.

Any Successor Trustee shall possess all powers and responsibilities granted to the original Trustee under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE III  
### BENEFICIARIES

During the Grantor's lifetime, the Grantor shall be the primary beneficiary of this Trust.

Upon the Grantor's death, the remaining trust estate shall be distributed as follows:

1. **Elizabeth Carrington**, the Grantor's daughter, shall receive forty percent (40%) of the remaining trust estate.
2. **Nicholas Fitzpatrick**, the Grantor's son, shall receive thirty-five percent (35%) of the remaining trust estate.
3. **Madeleine Harrington**, the Grantor's niece, shall receive fifteen percent (15%) of the remaining trust estate.
4. **Christopher Beauregard**, the Grantor's nephew, shall receive ten percent (10%) of the remaining trust estate.

If any beneficiary predeceases the Grantor, that beneficiary's share shall pass to his or her descendants, per stirpes. If no descendants survive, that share shall be distributed proportionately among the remaining beneficiaries.

## ARTICLE IV  
### REVOCATION AND AMENDMENT

During the Grantor's lifetime and while possessing legal capacity, the Grantor reserves the unrestricted right to amend, modify, or revoke this Agreement, in whole or in part.

Any amendment or revocation shall be made in writing and delivered to the acting Trustee.

## ARTICLE V  
### DISTRIBUTIONS DURING GRANTOR'S LIFETIME

During the Grantor's lifetime, the Trustee shall distribute to or for the benefit of the Grantor as much of the income and principal of the Trust as the Grantor may request.

The Trustee may also distribute such amounts as the Trustee deems necessary for the Grantor's health, education, maintenance, and support.

## ARTICLE VI  
### TRUSTEE POWERS

The Trustee shall have all powers reasonably necessary to administer the Trust, including the authority to:

1. Purchase, sell, exchange, and manage real and personal property.
2. Invest and reinvest trust assets.
3. Open and maintain financial accounts.
4. Pay taxes, expenses, and administrative costs.
5. Employ attorneys, accountants, investment advisors, and other professionals.
6. Make distributions to beneficiaries in accordance with this Agreement.

The Trustee shall exercise all powers in good faith and in accordance with applicable law.

## ARTICLE VII  
### INCAPACITY

If the Grantor becomes incapacitated, as determined by written certification from two licensed physicians, the Successor Trustee shall assume responsibility for administering the Trust.

The Successor Trustee shall use trust assets for the Grantor's care, maintenance, and general welfare.

## ARTICLE VIII  
### GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Maryland.

## ARTICLE IX  
### MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

The headings contained herein are for organizational purposes only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X
