# THE WHITAKER FAMILY REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST
## REVOCABLE TRUST AGREEMENT

**Date of Execution:** August 21, 2026  
**Governing Law:** District of Columbia

This Revocable Trust Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on August 21, 2026, by and between **Daniel Robert Whitaker**, residing at 1842 Connecticut Avenue NW, Washington, D.C. 20009 (hereinafter referred to as the "Grantor" or "Settlor"), and **Margaret Elaine Collins**, residing at 2200 Massachusetts Avenue NW, Washington, D.C. 20008 (hereinafter referred to as the "Initial Trustee").

The Grantor hereby establishes the Whitaker Family Revocable Living Trust ("Trust") and transfers, assigns, and delivers to the Trustee the property described in Schedule A attached hereto, together with any additional property subsequently transferred to or acquired by the Trust.

The Trustee agrees to hold, manage, invest, and distribute the Trust Estate in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth herein.

The Grantor declares that this Trust is intended to constitute a valid revocable inter vivos trust under the laws of the District of Columbia.

---

# ARTICLE I. CREATION AND IDENTIFICATION OF THE TRUST

### Section 1.01. Name of Trust

The trust established pursuant to this Agreement shall be known as the "Whitaker Family Revocable Living Trust dated August 21, 2026."

All property transferred to the Trustee shall be held, administered, and distributed under this name, or under any substantially similar designation sufficient to identify the Trust.

### Section 1.02. Declaration of Trust

The Grantor hereby establishes this Trust for the purposes of:

(a) Providing for the Grantor's financial support and maintenance during the Grantor's lifetime;

(b) Providing for the Grantor's spouse and descendants following the Grantor's death;

(c) Preserving and managing Trust assets during periods of incapacity;

(d) Minimizing unnecessary probate administration;

(e) Providing for the orderly distribution of Trust assets upon the Grantor's death; and

(f) Establishing continuing trusts for designated beneficiaries to protect inherited assets and provide for their long-term financial security.

### Section 1.03. Initial Trust Property

The initial Trust Estate shall consist of the property identified in Schedule A, incorporated herein by reference.

The Grantor may subsequently transfer additional real property, personal property, securities, financial accounts, business interests, and other transferable assets to the Trust.

No property shall become Trust property merely because it is listed in an unattached schedule unless ownership is effectively transferred or otherwise declared to be held in trust.

### Section 1.04. Acceptance by Trustee

The Initial Trustee accepts appointment and agrees to administer the Trust in accordance with this Agreement and applicable District of Columbia law.

### Section 1.05. Separate Property

Property contributed by the Grantor shall retain its character as property attributable to the Grantor for purposes of identifying the source of contributions and administering the Trust.

Nothing herein shall be construed to alter the ownership rights of any person who has not validly transferred property to the Trust.

---

# ARTICLE II. REVOCATION AND AMENDMENT

### Section 2.01. Reserved Power of Revocation

During the Grantor's lifetime and while possessing the requisite legal capacity, the Grantor reserves the absolute right to revoke this Trust, in whole or in part.

Upon revocation, the Trustee shall distribute the affected Trust property as directed in writing by the Grantor.

### Section 2.02. Power to Amend

The Grantor reserves the right to amend, modify, supplement, or restate any provision of this Agreement.

An amendment shall be made by a written instrument signed by the Grantor and delivered to the acting Trustee.

The Trustee shall retain all amendments with the original Trust Agreement.

### Section 2.03. Exclusive Method of Amendment

The Grantor intends that the written procedure described in this Article constitute the exclusive method of amendment or revocation to the extent permitted by applicable law.

No oral statement, informal communication, or purported amendment shall modify this Agreement.

### Section 2.04. Amendment Following Incapacity

No amendment or revocation may be made by the Grantor after the Grantor has been determined incapacitated under Article IV, except as otherwise authorized by a court of competent jurisdiction.

An agent acting under a power of attorney may exercise the Grantor's powers only to the extent expressly authorized by the terms of this Trust or the applicable power of attorney and permitted by law.

### Section 2.05. Irrevocability Upon Death

Upon the Grantor's death, this Trust shall become irrevocable.

No beneficiary, Trustee, or other interested person shall possess the authority to amend or revoke the Trust except as expressly permitted under this Agreement or applicable law.

---

# ARTICLE III. BENEFICIARIES

### Section 3.01. Primary Beneficiary

During the Grantor's lifetime, the Grantor, Daniel Robert Whitaker, shall be the primary beneficiary of the Trust.

The Trustee shall administer the Trust primarily for the Grantor's benefit.

### Section 3.02. Spousal Beneficiary

The Grantor designates his spouse, **Eleanor Grace Whitaker**, residing at 1842 Connecticut Avenue NW, Washington, D.C. 20009, as the secondary beneficiary.

Upon the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall administer the Trust Estate for the benefit of the surviving spouse as provided in Article VII.

### Section 3.03. Descendant Beneficiaries

The Grantor identifies the following children as beneficiaries:

1. **Alexander James Whitaker**, born March 14, 2002.
2. **Charlote Elizabeth Whitaker**, born September 8, 2005.

The term "descendants" shall include the Grantor's lineal descendants, whether born before or after the execution of this Agreement, including legally adopted descendants.

Unless otherwise expressly provided, descendants shall take by right of representation, per stirpes.

### Section 3.04. Contingent Beneficiaries

If the Grantor, the Grantor's spouse, and all descendants of the Grantor predecease the final distribution of the Trust Estate, the remaining property shall be distributed to the Grantor's heirs at law as determined under applicable District of Columbia intestacy law.

### Section 3.05. Exclusion of Other Persons

Except as expressly provided herein, no individual shall have a beneficial interest in the Trust merely by virtue of being related to the Grantor or being named in another estate-planning document.

---

# ARTICLE IV. INCAPACITY OF THE GRANTOR

### Section 4.01. Determination of Incapacity

The Grantor shall be considered incapacitated if the Grantor is unable to manage financial affairs or communicate responsible decisions concerning financial matters due to physical or mental impairment.

Incapacity shall be established by written certifications from two licensed physicians, at least one of whom shall be familiar with the Grantor's medical condition.

If the Grantor's condition prevents obtaining two certifications without unreasonable delay, a court of competent jurisdiction may determine incapacity.

### Section 4.02. Appointment of Successor Trustee During Incapacity

Upon a determination of incapacity, the Initial Trustee shall continue administering the Trust.

If the Initial Trustee is unable or unwilling to serve, **Robert Michael Bennett**, residing at 1600 K Street NW, Washington, D.C. 20006, shall assume the office of Successor Trustee.

### Section 4.03. Distributions During Incapacity

During the Grantor's incapacity, the Trustee shall distribute such amounts of income and principal as the Trustee determines reasonably necessary for:

(a) The Grantor's health and medical care;

(b) Housing, maintenance, and personal expenses;

(c) Nursing care, rehabilitation, and assisted living;

(d) Insurance premiums and taxes;

(e) The support and maintenance of the Grantor's spouse and dependent children; and

(f) Other expenses consistent with the Grantor's accustomed standard of living.

### Section 4.04. Preservation of Estate Plan

During incapacity, the Trustee shall make reasonable efforts to preserve the Grantor's estate plan, including maintaining insurance, paying recurring obligations, and managing assets prudently.

The Trustee shall not make extraordinary gifts or transfers to beneficiaries except as expressly authorized by this Agreement or applicable law.

### Section 4.05. Restoration of Capacity

If the Grantor regains capacity, the Grantor shall resume control of the Trust.

Restoration shall be established through written medical certifications substantially consistent with the procedure established in Section 4.01.

### Section 5.02. Successor Trustees

The following individuals shall serve in the order listed if the preceding Trustee dies, resigns, becomes incapacitated, or is removed:

Second Successor Trustee: **Patricia Ann Sullivan**, residing at 900 16th Street NW, Washington, D.C. 20006.

Third Successor Trustee: **First National Fiduciary Services, Inc.**, a fictional corporate fiduciary.

### Section 5.03. Acceptance of Appointment

A successor Trustee shall accept appointment by executing a written acceptance and delivering it to the appropriate beneficiaries or their representatives.

Upon acceptance, the successor Trustee shall assume all fiduciary responsibilities and powers granted under this Agreement.

### Section 5.04. Resignation

A Trustee may resign by providing at least thirty (30) days' written notice to the Grantor, if living and competent, or otherwise to the qualified beneficiaries.

The resigning Trustee shall provide a complete accounting and transfer all Trust property, records, and relevant documentation to the successor Trustee.

For purposes of this Agreement, the Initial Trustee's appointment shall be deemed effective as of August 21, 2025.

### Section 5.05. Removal

During the Grantor's lifetime and while competent, the Grantor may remove any Trustee, with or without cause, by written notice.

Following the Grantor's death, a Trustee may be removed by a court of competent jurisdiction or through any other procedure permitted under applicable law.

### Section 5.06. Bond

No Trustee shall be required to furnish bond or other security unless ordered by a court of competent jurisdiction.

### Section 5.07. Compensation

An individual Trustee shall be entitled to reasonable compensation for services rendered, taking into consideration the complexity of the Trust, time expended, and customary fiduciary compensation.

A corporate Trustee shall receive compensation according to its published fee schedule or a written agreement approved by the Grantor or otherwise authorized under applicable law.

### Section 5.08. Reimbursement

A Trustee shall be reimbursed for reasonable expenses properly incurred in administering, protecting, and preserving the Trust Estate.

# ARTICLE VI. TRUSTEE POWERS AND FIDUCIARY DUTIES

### Section 6.01. General Authority

The Trustee shall possess all powers reasonably necessary to administer the Trust and carry out its purposes, subject to the fiduciary duties imposed by applicable law.

### Section 6.02. Investment Authority

The Trustee may invest and reinvest Trust property in:

(a) Stocks, bonds, and mutual funds;

(b) Exchange-traded funds;

(c) Government securities;

(d) Certificates of deposit;

(e) Real property;

(f) Closely held business interests;

(g) Private investment funds; and

(h) Other investments deemed appropriate under the circumstances.

The Trustee shall consider the purposes, distribution requirements, liquidity needs, and risk profile of the Trust when making investment decisions.

### Section 6.03. Real Property

The Trustee may acquire, retain, improve, lease, mortgage, sell, or otherwise manage real property.

The Trustee may employ property managers, contractors, attorneys, and other professionals when reasonably necessary.

### Section 6.04. Business Interests

The Trustee may retain ownership interests in any business transferred to the Trust.

The Trustee may exercise voting rights, appoint managers, approve distributions, and participate in reorganizations or sales, subject to fiduciary obligations.

### Section 6.05. Banking Authority

The Trustee may establish and maintain bank, brokerage, and investment accounts in the name of the Trust.

The Trustee may deposit, withdraw, transfer, and invest funds as reasonably necessary to administer the Trust.

### Section 6.06. Borrowing Authority

The Trustee may borrow money on behalf of the Trust and pledge Trust assets as collateral when reasonably necessary for Trust administration or preservation.

### Section 6.07. Employment of Professionals

The Trustee may retain attorneys, accountants, financial advisers, appraisers, property managers, and other professionals.

Reasonable professional fees shall be payable from the Trust Estate.

### Section 6.08. Insurance

The Trustee may purchase and maintain insurance covering Trust property, including liability, casualty, and other appropriate coverage.

### Section 6.09. Tax Matters

The Trustee may prepare and file applicable federal and District of Columbia tax returns, make elections permitted by law, pay tax liabilities, and retain professionals to advise on tax matters.

### Section 6.10. Duty of Loyalty

The Trustee shall administer the Trust solely in the interests of its beneficiaries, subject to the special rights reserved to the Grantor during the Trust's revocable period.

The Trustee shall avoid self-dealing and unauthorized conflicts of interest.

### Section 6.11. Duty of Prudence

The Trustee shall exercise reasonable care, skill, and caution in administering the Trust.

### Section 6.12. Records and Accounting

The Trustee shall maintain accurate financial records, including receipts, disbursements, investment transactions, and distributions.

After the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall provide accountings to qualified beneficiaries as required by applicable law and this Agreement.

---

# ARTICLE VII. DISTRIBUTION UPON THE GRANTOR'S DEATH

### Section 7.01. Administrative Period

Upon the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall collect and secure Trust property, determine outstanding obligations, obtain valuations where appropriate, and administer the Trust Estate before making final distributions.

### Section 7.02. Payment of Expenses

The Trustee may pay from the Trust Estate:

(a) Funeral and burial expenses;

(b) Reasonable expenses of administering the Trust;

(c) Enforceable debts and obligations of the Grantor;

(d) Taxes properly payable by the Trust or attributable to the Grantor's estate; and

(e) Other expenses necessary to preserve and distribute Trust property.

The Trustee shall exercise discretion in determining which assets should be used to satisfy such obligations.

### Section 7.03. Specific Gifts

Following payment or reasonable provision for expenses and liabilities, the Trustee shall distribute:

(a) $100,000 to the Grantor's spouse, Eleanor Grace Whitaker;

(b) $50,000 to Alexander James Whitaker; and

(c) $50,000 to Charlotte Elizabeth Whitaker.

If a named beneficiary does not survive the Grantor by thirty (30) days, the applicable gift shall lapse and become part of the residuary Trust Estate unless otherwise provided by law.

### Section 7.04. Distribution of Residue

The remaining Trust Estate shall be divided into two equal shares:

(a) Fifty percent (50%) for the benefit of Eleanor Grace Whitaker; and

(b) Fifty percent (50%) collectively for the benefit of the Grantor's descendants.

The spouse's share shall be administered under Article VIII.

The descendants' share shall be administered under Article IX.

### Section 7.05. Simultaneous Death

If the Grantor and spouse die under circumstances in which the order of death cannot be established, the spouse shall be deemed to have predeceased the Grantor for purposes of this Agreement.

The Trust Estate shall then be distributed in accordance with the provisions governing the Grantor's descendants.

### Section 7.06. Thirty-Day Survival Requirement

A beneficiary must survive the Grantor by thirty (30) calendar days to receive a distribution under this Article.

A beneficiary who fails to satisfy this requirement shall be treated as having predeceased the Grantor.

---

# ARTICLE VIII. SURVIVING SPOUSE'S TRUST

### Section 8.01. Establishment

Upon the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall establish a separate trust known as the "Whitaker Spousal Continuing Trust."

The Trustee shall allocate fifty percent (50%) of the residuary Trust Estate to this trust, subject to the payment of specific gifts and expenses.

### Section 8.02. Income Distributions

The Trustee shall distribute the net income of the Spousal Continuing Trust to Eleanor Grace Whitaker at least annually.

### Section 8.03. Principal Distributions

The Trustee may distribute principal to or for the benefit of the surviving spouse as the Trustee determines necessary or appropriate for the spouse's:

(a) Health;

(b) Education;

(c) Maintenance; and

(d) Support.

### Section 8.04. Principal Preservation

In making discretionary distributions, the Trustee shall consider the surviving spouse's other reasonably available resources, anticipated expenses, and standard of living.

The Trustee shall also consider the interests of the Grantor's descendants as remainder beneficiaries.

### Section 8.05. Residence

If the Grantor's primary residence is held in the Trust at death, the surviving spouse shall have the right to occupy the residence rent-free during the spouse's lifetime, provided that the spouse remains responsible for ordinary occupancy expenses, including utilities and routine maintenance.

The Trustee shall pay extraordinary structural expenses from the Trust Estate when reasonably appropriate.

The surviving spouse may voluntarily relinquish this occupancy right in writing.

### Section 8.06. Termination

Upon the death of the surviving spouse, the Spousal Continuing Trust shall terminate.

The remaining principal and undistributed income shall be distributed to the Grantor's descendants, per stirpes, and administered under Article IX.

---

# ARTICLE IX. DESCENDANTS' CONTINUING TRUSTS

### Section 9.01. Separate Shares

The Trustee shall divide the descendants' share into equal portions for the Grantor's then-living children.

If a child predeceases the Grantor leaving descendants, that child's share shall pass to those descendants by right of representation.

### Section 9.02. Separate Beneficiary Trusts

Each beneficiary's share shall be held as a separate continuing trust.

The Trustee shall maintain separate records and accounting for each beneficiary.

### Section 9.03. Discretionary Distributions

The Trustee may distribute income and principal for a beneficiary's health, education, maintenance, and support.

Permitted expenditures may include:

(a) Undergraduate and graduate education;

(b) Vocational training;

(c) Medical and dental expenses;

(d) Housing and living expenses;

(e) Reasonable transportation expenses;

(f) Professional development; and

(g) Other expenditures consistent with the beneficiary's welfare.

### Section 9.04. Age-Based Principal Distributions

Subject to the Trustee's determination that the beneficiary is capable of managing the distributed property, each beneficiary shall receive:

(a) One-third (1/3) of the beneficiary's trust share upon reaching age thirty (30);

(b) One-third (1/3) of the beneficiary's original trust share upon reaching age thirty-five (35); and

(c) The remaining balance upon reaching age forty (40).

Any prior discretionary distributions shall not automatically reduce these scheduled distributions unless expressly accounted for as an advancement under a written determination by the Trustee.

### Section 9.05. Delayed Distribution

If a beneficiary is experiencing substance dependency, financial exploitation, severe creditor difficulties, or another condition that presents a substantial risk to the beneficiary's financial welfare, the Trustee may petition a court or exercise any available lawful discretionary authority to preserve the assets rather than make an unsafe distribution.

The Trustee shall not postpone a mandatory distribution merely because the Trustee disagrees with the beneficiary's lifestyle or personal choices.

### Section 9.06. Death Before Full Distribution

If a beneficiary dies before receiving the entire trust share, the remaining balance shall be distributed to that beneficiary's descendants, per stirpes.

If the beneficiary leaves no descendants, the remaining balance shall be divided among the surviving descendants of the Grantor, per stirpes.

### Section 9.07. Beneficiary's Power of Appointment

Upon reaching age forty (40), a beneficiary may exercise a limited testamentary power of appointment over the remaining trust property.

The beneficiary may appoint the remaining property among the Grantor's descendants and qualifying charitable organizations.

The beneficiary shall not appoint the property to the beneficiary, the beneficiary's estate, the beneficiary's creditors, or creditors of the beneficiary's estate.

The power must be exercised through a valid will or other testamentary instrument that specifically refers to this power.

### Section 9.08. Termination of Beneficiary Trust

A beneficiary trust shall terminate upon complete distribution of its principal and accumulated income.

---

# ARTICLE X. MINOR AND INCAPACITATED BENEFICIARIES

### Section 10.01. Distributions to Minors

If a beneficiary is under the age of eighteen (18), the Trustee may make distributions:

(a) Directly to the beneficiary for appropriate expenses;

(b) To a parent or legal guardian;

(c) To a custodian under the applicable Uniform Transfers to Minors Act; or

(d) To a separate trust established for the beneficiary.

### Section 10.02. Incapacitated Beneficiaries

If a beneficiary becomes incapacitated, the Trustee may retain that beneficiary's share in trust and make distributions for the beneficiary's benefit.

### Section 10.03. Special Needs Considerations

If a beneficiary receives or may qualify for means-tested public benefits, the Trustee may establish or maintain a separate supplemental needs trust where legally appropriate.

The Trustee shall avoid distributions that unnecessarily jeopardize eligibility for public assistance.

### Section 10.04. Protective Administration

The Trustee may make payments directly to service providers rather than distributing funds directly to a beneficiary when doing so is reasonably necessary to protect the beneficiary's interests.

---

# ARTICLE XI. SPENDTHRIFT PROVISIONS

### Section 11.01. Restriction on Transfers

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, no beneficiary may voluntarily assign, transfer, pledge, or otherwise dispose of an interest in undistributed Trust property.

### Section 11.02. Creditor Protection

To the maximum extent permitted by law, a beneficiary's interest shall not be subject to attachment, execution, garnishment, or other creditor process before actual distribution.

### Section 11.03. Trustee's Discretion

The Trustee may withhold discretionary distributions if the Trustee reasonably determines that a proposed distribution would be immediately diverted to creditors or would otherwise undermine the purposes of the Trust.

### Section 11.04. Exceptions

Nothing in this Article shall be construed to defeat any mandatory creditor rights or statutory exceptions recognized under District of Columbia law.

---

# ARTICLE XII. TAXES AND ADMINISTRATIVE EXPENSES

### Section 12.01. Tax Returns

The Trustee shall cause all required federal and District of Columbia tax returns to be prepared and filed.

### Section 12.02. Tax Elections

The Trustee may make tax elections available under applicable federal and District of Columbia law when reasonably advisable.

### Section 12.03. Allocation of Taxes

Estate taxes and other death-related taxes attributable to Trust property shall be allocated among the appropriate interests in accordance with applicable law and the terms of this Agreement.

The Trustee shall consider available deductions, exemptions, and elections before making allocations.

### Section 12.04. Professional Assistance

The Trustee may retain qualified tax professionals and attorneys to assist in tax administration.

### Section 12.05. Tax Apportionment

The Trustee shall seek equitable apportionment of taxes among beneficiaries and assets to the extent permitted by law, taking into account any specific tax allocation provisions applicable to particular gifts or property.

---

# ARTICLE XIII. TRUSTEE LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

### Section 13.01. Standard of Care

A Trustee shall not be liable for a loss arising from the proper exercise of fiduciary discretion, provided that the Trustee acted in good faith and in accordance with applicable fiduciary duties.

### Section 13.02. Liability for Breach

A Trustee shall remain liable for losses resulting from fraud, willful misconduct, bad faith, or a breach of fiduciary duty.

Nothing herein shall waive liability to the extent such waiver is prohibited by District of Columbia law.

### Section 13.03. Indemnification

The Trust may indemnify a Trustee for reasonable expenses and liabilities incurred in good-faith administration of the Trust.

Indemnification shall not extend to losses arising from the Trustee's bad faith, reckless indifference, or other conduct for which indemnification is prohibited by law.

### Section 13.04. Reliance on Professional Advice

A Trustee may rely in good faith on professional advice obtained from attorneys, accountants, appraisers, and financial advisers, provided the Trustee exercises reasonable care in selecting and supervising those professionals.

---

# ARTICLE XIV. TRUST ADMINISTRATION AND ACCOUNTING

### Section 14.01. Accounting Period

The Trustee shall maintain an accounting period ending on December 31 of each calendar year.

### Section 14.02. Annual Reports

Following the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall provide annual reports to qualified beneficiaries as required by applicable law.

### Section 14.03. Inspection Rights

Qualified beneficiaries shall have the right to request information concerning Trust administration to the extent provided under District of Columbia law.

### Section 14.04. Notices

Any notice required under this Agreement shall be delivered personally, by certified mail, or by another reliable method reasonably calculated to provide actual notice.

### Section 14.05. Confidentiality

The Trustee shall take reasonable steps to protect confidential Trust information, subject to disclosure requirements imposed by law.

---

# ARTICLE XV. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND CONSTRUCTION

### Section 15.01. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the District of Columbia.

### Section 15.02. Jurisdiction

Any judicial proceeding concerning the administration, interpretation, or validity of this Trust shall be brought before a court of competent jurisdiction in the District of Columbia, subject to applicable jurisdictional requirements.

### Section 15.03. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain effective to the fullest extent permitted by law.

### Section 15.04. Construction

Words in the singular shall include the plural where appropriate.

References to a particular gender shall include all genders.

Headings are included for organizational purposes and shall not independently determine the meaning of any provision.

### Section 15.05. No-Contest Provision

To the extent permitted by District of Columbia law, a beneficiary who directly contests the validity of this Trust without probable cause may forfeit the beneficiary's interest, with the forfeited property passing as though the beneficiary had predeceased the Grantor.

This provision shall not apply to a petition brought in good faith to interpret the Trust, obtain an accounting, or remedy a Trustee's breach of fiduciary duty.

# ARTICLE XVI. TERMINATION OF TRUST

### Section 16.01. General Termination

The Trust shall terminate upon the complete distribution of all Trust property in accordance with this Agreement.

### Section 16.02. Early Termination

If the Trustee determines that continued administration of a separate beneficiary trust is uneconomical or impracticable, the Trustee may terminate that trust and distribute its assets in a manner consistent with its purposes, subject to applicable law and any required court approval.

### Section 16.03. Final Accounting

Before final termination, the Trustee shall prepare a final accounting and provide it to the appropriate beneficiaries.

### Section 16.04. Distribution Upon Termination

Upon termination, all remaining Trust property shall be distributed to the persons entitled to receive it under the applicable distribution provisions.

### Section 17.03. Digital Assets

The Trustee may access, manage, preserve, transfer, and dispose of digital assets belonging to the Trust to the extent authorized by applicable law.

### Section 17.04. Corporate Trustee

If a corporate Trustee is appointed, the corporate Trustee shall possess all powers granted to an individual Trustee, subject to its governing documents and applicable law.

### Section 17.05. Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be treated as an original to the extent permitted by law.

### Section 17.06. Electronic Records

Electronic copies of this Agreement and its amendments may be maintained for administrative purposes. The Trustee shall preserve the original executed instrument and any original documents required by law.
