# WHITAKER FAMILY REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST
**DATED NOVEMBER 20, 2011**

This Revocable Living Trust Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into on November 20, 2011, by and between **Daniel Robert Whitaker**, of Washington, District of Columbia (the “Grantor”), and **Daniel Robert Whitaker**, as initial Trustee (the “Trustee”).

The Grantor hereby establishes this revocable trust, to be known as the **Whitaker Family Revocable Living Trust dated November 20, 2011** (the “Trust”).

## ARTICLE I
### ESTABLISHMENT OF TRUST

**1.1 Creation of Trust.** The Grantor hereby transfers, assigns, and delivers to the Trustee the property described in Schedule A attached hereto, together with any additional property that may subsequently be transferred to the Trust.

**1.2 Name of Trust.** This Trust shall be known as the Whitaker Family Revocable Living Trust dated November 20, 2001.

**1.3 Declaration of Intent.** The Grantor intends to establish a revocable living trust for the management, preservation, and distribution of his property during his lifetime and upon his death.

**1.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the District of Columbia.

## ARTICLE II
### FAMILY INFORMATION

**2.1 Grantor's Family.** The Grantor is married to **Margaret Anne Whiteker**.

**2.2 Children.** The Grantor has two children:

1. Michael Daniel Whitaker.
2. Charlotte Elizabeth Whitaker.

References to the Grantor's children shall include the individuals identified above and any additional children subsequently born to or legally adopted by the Grantor.

## ARTICLE III
### REVOCATION AND AMENDMENT

**3.1 Right to Revoke.** During the Grantor's lifetime, the Grantor reserves the unrestricted right to revoke this Trust, in whole or in part, by delivering a written instrument of revocation to the Trustee.

**3.2 Right to Amend.** The Grantor may amend or modify any provision of this Agreement by executing a written instrument identifying the amendment and delivering it to the Trustee.

**3.3 Exercise of Rights.** The powers of revocation and amendment reserved under this Article shall be exercised personally by the Grantor.

## ARTICLE IV
### TRUSTEE AND SUCCESSOR TRUSTEES

**4.1 Initial Trustee.** Daniel Robert Whitaker shall serve as the initial Trustee of this Trust.

**4.2 Successor Trustee.** Upon the resignation, incapacity, or death of the initial Trustee, Margaret Anne Whiteker shall serve as Successor Trustee.

**4.3 Alternate Successor Trustee.** If Margaret Anne Whiteker is unable or unwilling to serve, Michael Daniel Whitaker shall serve as the next Successor Trustee.

**4.4 Trustee's Authority.** The Trustee shall have all powers reasonably necessary to administer the Trust, including the authority to:

(a) Purchase, sell, exchange, and lease real and personal property;

(b) Invest and reinvest Trust assets;

(c) Open and maintain financial accounts;

(d) Pay taxes, expenses, and debts properly chargeable to the Trust;

(e) Employ attorneys, accountants, investment advisors, and other professionals; and

(f) Distribute Trust property in accordance with this Agreement.

## ARTICLE V
### DISTRIBUTIONS DURING THE GRANTOR'S LIFETIME

**5.1 Distributions to Grantor.** During the Grantor's lifetime, the Trustee shall distribute to or for the benefit of the Grantor such amounts of income and principal as the Grantor may request.

**5.2 Discretionary Distributions.** The Trustee may distribute income or principal to the Grantor for his health, education, maintenance, and support.

**5.3 Retention of Income.** Any income not distributed shall be accumulated and added to the principal of the Trust.

## ARTICLE VI
### DISTRIBUTIONS UPON THE GRANTOR'S DEATH

**6.1 Payment of Expenses.** Upon the Grantor's death, the Trustee may pay the Grantor's enforceable debts, funeral expenses, expenses of administering the Trust, and any applicable taxes.

**6.2 Distribution to Surviving Spouse.** If Margaret Anne Whiteker survives the Grantor, the Trustee shall distribute fifty percent (50%) of the remaining Trust estate to her outright and free of trust.

**6.3 Distribution to Children.** The remaining fifty percent (50%) of the Trust estate shall be divided into equal shares for the Grantor's children who survive him.

**6.4 Deceased Child's Descendants.** If either child predeceases the Grantor but leaves descendants surviving the Grantor, that child's share shall pass to such descendants by right of representation.

## ARTICLE VII
### TRUSTS FOR DESCENDANTS

**7.1 Continuing Trusts.** Any property allocated to a descendant who has not attained the age of thirty (30) years shall be held in a separate continuing trust.

**7.2 Discretionary Distributions.** The Trustee may distribute income and principal for the beneficiary's health, education, maintenance, and support.

**7.3 Distribution at Age Thirty.** Upon attaining thirty (30) years of age, the beneficiary shall receive the remaining principal and undistributed income of his or her separate trust.

**7.4 Death Before Final Distribution.** If a beneficiary dies before receiving the entire trust estate, the remaining property shall be distributed to that beneficiary's descendants, by right of representation, or, if none, to the Grantor's then-living descendants.

## ARTICLE VIII
### SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

**8.1 Restriction on Transfers.** Before actual distribution, no beneficiary shall have the right to voluntarily or involuntarily assign, transfer, pledge, or otherwise encumber any interest in the Trust.

**8.2 Creditor Protection.** To the extent permitted by applicable law, no interest of a beneficiary shall be subject to attachment, execution, or claims of creditors before distribution.

## ARTICLE IX
### TRUSTEE ADMINISTRATION

**9.1 Records.** The Trustee shall maintain accurate books and records reflecting all receipts, disbursements, investments, and distributions.

**9.2 Accounting.** The Trustee shall provide accountings to qualified beneficiaries as required by applicable law.

**9.3 Compensation.** The Trustee shall be entitled to reasonable compensation for services performed in administering the Trust and reimbursement for reasonable expenses incurred.

**9.4 Bond.** No Trustee serving under this Agreement shall be required to furnish a bond unless required by a court of competent jurisdiction.

## ARTICLE X
### MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

**10.1 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.2 Headings.** Article and section headings are included solely for convenience and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

**10.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding concerning the establishment and administration of this Trust.

**10.4 Effective Date.** This Trust shall become effective upon execution by the Grantor and initial Trustee on November 20, 2011.
