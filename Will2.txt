# LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF NEIL CARTER

**LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT**

I, Neal Carter, a resident of Washington, District of Columbia, being of sound mind and disposing memory, do hereby declare this document to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils previously made by me.

## ARTICLE I – IDENTIFICATION AND DECLARATION

I am currently domiciled in Washington, District of Columbia. I am unmarried and have two children, Michael Carter and Elizabeth Carter.

I declare that this Will expresses my final wishes concerning the distribution of my estate and the appointment of fiduciaries to administer my affairs following my death.

This Last Will and Testament is executed on August 21, 2026.

## ARTICLE II – APPOINTMENT OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I appoint my brother, James Carter, as Personal Representative of my estate. If James Carter is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint my sister, Sarah Mitchell, as successor Personal Representative.

My Personal Representative shall have all powers permitted under applicable District of Columbia law, including the authority to collect assets, pay debts, administer property, and distribute my estate.

## ARTICLE III – PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Personal Representative to pay all legally enforceable debts, funeral expenses, expenses of last illness, and reasonable costs associated with the administration of my estate.

These payments should be made as soon as reasonably practicable following my death.

## ARTICLE IV – DISTRIBUTION OF PERSONAL PROPERTY

I give my personal jewelry, watches, and other personal effects to my daughter, Elizabeth Carter.

I give my automobile and any associated accessories to my son, Michael Carter.

Any personal property not specifically addressed in this Article shall be distributed equally between my children.

## ARTICLE V – DISTRIBUTION OF RESIDUARY ESTATE

I direct that the remainder of my estate, including all real property, financial accounts, investments, and other assets not otherwise distributed under this Will, shall be divided equally between my two children, Michael Carter and Elizabeth Carter.

If either child predeceases me, that child's share shall pass to his or her surviving descendants, per stirpes.

## ARTICLE VI – GUARDIANSHIP

If either of my children is a minor at the time of my death, I nominate my sister, Sarah Mitchell, to serve as guardian of the minor child's person and property, subject to court approval.

## ARTICLE VII – GENERAL PROVISIONS

If any provision of this Will is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect to the extent permitted by law.

I direct that this Will be interpreted and administered in accordance with the laws of the District of Columbia.

## ARTICLE VIII – EXECUTION

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I, Neil Carter, have signed this Last Will and Testament on August 12, 2026, declaring it to be my Last Will and Testament.
